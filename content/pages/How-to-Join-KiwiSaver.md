faq_items:
  - question: Who can join KiwiSaver?
    answer: |-
      KiwiSaver is open to New Zealand citizens, and to anyone entitled to live in New Zealand indefinitely, who lives or normally lives here. There’s no upper age limit, so you can join at 65 or older. From 65, you won’t get the government contribution, and your employer doesn’t have to contribute, but you can still make your own contributions.

      If you’re self-employed, between jobs or returning from overseas, you can still join. You just won’t get employer contributions unless you’re employed.
  - question: Should I join KiwiSaver?
    answer: |
      For most people in New Zealand, yes. If you're employed, your employer has to contribute at least 3.5% of your gross pay on top of your own contributions. The government also adds 25 cents for every $1 you contribute, up to $260.72 a year, as long as you earn $180,000 or less.

      You won't get these extra contributions anywhere else. If money gets tight later, you can apply for a savings suspension to pause your contributions for a while.
  - question: How do I apply for KiwiSaver?
    answer: |
      Through BetterSaver, you can apply online in three steps:

      - Answer a few quick questions about your situation.
      - Review your recommended scheme.
      - Complete an application - we pass it to the provider to finalise.
  - question: Can I join KiwiSaver if I’m self-employed?
    answer: |
      Yes. If you're self-employed, you can join KiwiSaver and pay in whatever amount suits you, as often as you like. You won't get employer contributions, but you can still get the government contribution of up to $260.72 a year.

      To get the full amount, you need to contribute at least $1,042.86 between 1 July and 30 June, and earn $180,000 or less.
  - question: How do I open a KiwiSaver account without an employer?
    answer: 'You can apply directly through a KiwiSaver provider, or through BetterSaver. We’ll help you choose a scheme based on your situation, then guide you through the application online. You don’t need to be employed to join.'
  - question: What is the difference between joining and being automatically enrolled?
    answer: 'When you start a new job, your employer automatically enrols you in KiwiSaver if you’re not already a member. If you don’t choose a provider, you’re placed into a default scheme. Joining actively means you choose your provider from the start - which is what BetterSaver helps you do.'
  - question: How long does it take to join KiwiSaver?
    answer: 'Applying through BetterSaver takes most people under 5 minutes. Once your application is submitted, your provider will confirm your enrolment and your contributions will begin from your next pay.'
